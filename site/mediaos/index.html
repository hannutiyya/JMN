Sham MediaOS

The newsroom, website, apps and broadcast desk for media of every size

Sham MediaOS runs a whole news organisation on your own server: newsroom and CMS, a fast multilingual website, newsletters, reader payments, branded apps and, for broadcasters, rundowns, studios and live streaming. Under your own name.

See editions and pricesContact the team

One platform for the whole newsroom

Reporters file, editors review, the desk publishes and the website, newsletters, apps and feeds update together. Sham MediaOS replaces the patchwork of a CMS, a newsletter tool, a paywall plugin and a separate broadcast system with one installation that you own.

  • Newsroom and CMS with desks, roles, review steps, scheduling and a full revision history.
  • Fast multilingual website that works on slow connections and installs as an app (PWA).
  • 33 interface languages, including Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, Kiswahili and Arabic, with Ethiopian calendar dates.
  • Newsletters written and sent from the newsroom.
  • Branded mobile apps for Android and iOS, built under your own name and store accounts.
  • AI desk for translation drafts, summaries, transcripts and captions, always reviewed by a person.
  • Analytics, podcasts, elections results and polls.

Your brand from the first screen

Name, short name, tagline, colour and logo go in at installation and can be changed later. The website, e-mails, newsletters, the newsroom itself and your apps all follow. Readers and staff never see our name.

Readers pay the way they already pay

Visa and Mastercard through Stripe, PayPal, Chapa, telebirr, ArifPay and bank transfer, each paying into your own merchant account. Memberships, a paywall, one-off support, advertising and sponsored newsletters.

Built for broadcasters

The Broadcast edition adds rundowns, up to three studios, the programme schedule, on-air graphics, live streaming and relays, an interaction centre for calls and messages, and syndication with Content Credentials.

Installed on your server

One command on Ubuntu 24.04, or Docker, Kubernetes, or a browser installer for shared hosting. Backups, health checks and a go-live checklist are built in. Your content and your readers' data stay where you put them.

Secure by default

Two-step sign-in, passkeys and single sign-on (Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID and SAML 2.0 on Enterprise). Roles per desk, a tamper-evident audit log with signed exports, and signed licences.

Readers never lose access

If a licence lapses, the newsroom becomes read-only while the website, apps and feeds keep running. The daily licence check sends counts only, never your content.

Try it free for 14 days

Every new installation starts on the Professional edition for 14 days, no key needed. Buy a licence in the store when you are ready and paste the key in Administration → Licence; nothing is reinstalled.
